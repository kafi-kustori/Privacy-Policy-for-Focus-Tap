Privacy Policy for FocusTap

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Summary

FocusTap does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data We Don't Collect

  • Personal information — No name, email, phone number, or account details
  • App usage data — Which apps you open is detected in-memory only and never saved, logged, or transmitted
  • Location data — No GPS or location tracking
  • Device identifiers — No device ID, advertising ID, or IMEI access
  • Analytics or crash reports — No Firebase, no Crashlytics, no analytics SDKs
  • Cookies or tracking — No webviews, no hidden trackers

Data Stored on Your Device

FocusTap stores the following locally using Hive (on-device database):

  • Focus sessions — Your self-reported intention text, session duration, and timestamp
  • Protected apps list — The apps you've chosen to monitor
  • App settings — Your preferences and setup state

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never uploaded to any server. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Accessibility Service Details

What data is accessed: Only the package name of the app currently in the foreground (e.g. "com.instagram.android").

How the data is used: To detect when you open a protected app and trigger the intention overlay in real time. Data is processed in memory only — never saved, logged, or transmitted.

What is NOT accessed: No keystrokes, no screen content, no passwords, no personal information. The service only monitors app-switch events.

Technical details: canRetrieveWindowContent is set to false. Only TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED and TYPE_WINDOWS_CHANGED events are monitored.

Third-Party Services

FocusTap does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect or transmit data. All dependencies are offline-only.

Children's Privacy

FocusTap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access — All focus sessions and settings are visible within the app
  • Delete — Clear session history or remove protected apps at any time
  • Revoke permissions — Disable any permission in Android Settings
  • Uninstall — Removing the app deletes all stored data

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
