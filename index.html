Privacy Policy for Focus Tap

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

Focus Tap does not collect or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, so nothing you do in it can leave your device. Everything it stores stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data We Don't Collect

  • Personal information — No name, email, phone number, account or sign-in
  • Data sent off your device — None. Focus Tap does not request the internet permission and has no server.
  • Location data — No GPS or location access
  • Device identifiers — No device ID, advertising ID or IMEI access
  • Analytics, ads or crash reports — No ads, no Firebase, no Crashlytics, no analytics SDKs
  • Cookies or tracking — No webviews, no trackers

Data Stored on Your Device

Focus Tap stores the following only on your device, in a local database (Hive):

  • Focus sessions — For each time you set an intention: the protected app it was for (its name and package name), the intention you typed, the time you promised, when the session started and ended, and whether you kept to your time. This is what the History screen shows.
  • Protected apps list — The apps you chose to protect
  • Last intention per app — So the card can suggest your previous reason
  • Settings — Reminder style (bubble or notification) and setup state

This data is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete single sessions or clear all history in the app, and uninstalling Focus Tap deletes everything.

Accessibility Service Details

What data is accessed: Only the package name of the app currently in the foreground (for example "com.instagram.android").

How the data is used: To notice when you open an app you chose to protect and show the intention card. Apps you did not protect are checked in memory and forgotten. When you set an intention, the protected app's name is saved with that session on your device so History can show it. Nothing is ever sent off your device.

What is NOT accessed: No screen content, no keystrokes, no passwords, no messages, no personal information.

Technical details: canRetrieveWindowContent is false, and only TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED events are received. Release builds of the app write no app names to the system log.

Your choice: You are asked for consent before turning it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

Third-Party Libraries

Focus Tap is built with Flutter and uses these open-source libraries, all of which work offline and send no data: Hive and hive_flutter (local storage), flutter_overlay_window (the card and bubble), path_provider (finding the app's storage folder), uuid (session IDs) and fl_chart (History charts). Focus Tap uses no third-party services or SDKs that collect or transmit data.

Children's Privacy

Focus Tap is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored only on your device, you are in full control:

  • Access — All sessions and settings are visible in the app
  • Delete — Delete single sessions or clear all history in History, and remove protected apps at any time
  • Revoke permissions — Turn off any permission in Android Settings
  • Uninstall — Removing the app deletes all stored data
